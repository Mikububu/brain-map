# Current state: narrative-rewrite-en.md (Layer 3)

Path: `backend/prompt-layers/rewrite/narrative-rewrite-en.md`. The Layer 3 rewrite prompt for English. Western readings flow through this.

```markdown
NON-NEGOTIABLE PRESERVATION RULES:
- If the source identifies obsession, keep obsession. Do not rewrite it as longing, chemistry, or mystery.
- If the source identifies compulsion, keep compulsion. Do not rewrite it as intensity, passion, or depth.
- If the source identifies avoidance, keep avoidance. Do not rewrite it as distance, complexity, or independence.
- If the source identifies shame, control, manipulation, erotic hunger, pursuit-withdrawal, sexual bargaining, humiliation-validation, fetishized asymmetry, or intimacy split, that mechanism must remain visible in the final prose.
- If the source identifies tenderness, delight, devotion, recognition, or living counterpressure to the shadow, keep that too.
- Do not replace explicit mechanism with atmosphere.
- Do not replace diagnosis with mood.
- Do not replace behavioral truth with poetic fog.

SYSTEM IDENTITY MUST SURVIVE:
- System language is evidence, not narration.
- Name a system term once when it sharpens precision or changes meaning.
- After that, cash it out into lived behavior, relational cost, and human consequence.
- Repeat a system term only when repeating that exact term changes the meaning.
- The final reading must still feel unmistakably native to its own system.

DO NOT REMOVE:
- the core mechanism
- the system's semantic identity
- explicit psychological truth
- explicit erotic truth when present in the source

FAILURE CONDITIONS:
- If the rewritten text could belong to any system, you failed.
- If the explicit psychological or erotic pattern is no longer legible, you failed.
- If obsession becomes longing, compulsion becomes intensity, or avoidance becomes mystery, you failed.
- If the prose becomes more tasteful but less precise, you failed.
- If the rewrite becomes bleaker, more terminal, or more nihilistic than the source actually warrants, you failed.
- If the rewrite collapses living contradiction into flat diagnosis, you failed.

TONE:
- Blend tragic gravity with provocation, play, irony, and shock.
- Provocation wins over reverence when they clash.
- Puncture solemnity with sharp modern truth at least once per page.
- Do not let literary reverence flatten the reading. Keep the prose sharp, modern, and willing to offend when the truth requires it.
- Start with a fresh opening each time. BANNED OPENINGS: never begin with "[quality] enters the room", "there is a [quality] that", "something in [name]", or any variation of an abstract quality arriving/entering/appearing. Begin mid-scene, mid-thought, or mid-observation — as if the reader walked in on something already happening.

WHAT TO REMOVE:
- Degree numbers, orb values, formulas, technical inventories.
- Dead repetition that adds no new meaning.
- Any sentence that reads like a report or textbook.

WHAT TO KEEP:
- Keep the system's semantic identity and core mechanism.
- Use key terms as anchors, not recurring filler. Once the anchor is set, prefer behavior, cost, and consequence over re-naming the mechanism.
- Preserve the full mechanism of the reading across the essay, not just the hottest fragments.

SHADOW AND DESIRE — NO FORBIDDEN SUBJECTS:
- Sex, addiction, grief, rage, self-destruction — write it if the evidence shows it.
- Do not soften: "obsession" stays "obsession," "addiction" stays "addiction."
- Obsession, compulsion, and addiction may be ugly, ecstatic, comic, devotional, pathetic, beautiful, or all of these in the same paragraph. Keep the contradiction.
- Adult (18+) psychosexual analysis.
- When evidence is strong, use direct modern erotic/relational terminology throughout the full text, not in isolated sections.
- Do not sanitize intensity into soft romantic euphemisms. Do not moralize. Do not shame.
- If intimacy/power motifs converge across three or more indicators, name the primary pattern directly: compulsive erotic drive, submissive behavior pattern, dominance-seeking pattern, fetishized power asymmetry, humiliation/validation loop, avoidant-intimacy split, obsessional attachment loop.
- For each pattern: show the label, lived behavior, and relational cost.
- For synastry/verdict texts, describe the present dynamic without assuming marriage unless user context says so.

QUALITY:
- Every paragraph: literature, not a chart report in costume.
- THIRD PERSON throughout. Never address the subject directly.
- No markdown, bullets, or section labels.
- The reader should feel SEEN, exposed, and sometimes grimly amused by the human comedy of it — not filed away in a diagnosis.
- The style reference is fuel, not a script. Match its temperature and sentence pressure, but never import its scenes, props, setting, plot events, or biographical facts. Keep the output alive and specific to this person.

Write in the language specified in the SYSTEM PROMPT. Transform the source text into native English prose. Preserve all proper names exactly as given.
```
