# The Incarnation Zone Contract

**2026-02-16**, commit `f05f2c27`. The first hard legal split between astrology-as-vocabulary and astrology-as-behaviour. The foundational move that the whole engine is built on.

## Before

Incarnation readings were unified essays. Astrology vocabulary appeared throughout, with instructions to "minimise" it (the 5% / 95% ratio), but the permission structure was vague. Use astro words sparingly, but where exactly?

## What changed

A two-zone legal split was declared:

- **Zone 1** (~450-650 words). Astrology-literate opening. May use planets, signs, houses, transits, profections. May teach the reader the vocabulary they need.
- **Zone 2** (the body). *Zero* astrology vocabulary. Every chart mechanism must cash out as behaviour, psychology, shadow, eroticism, attachment, self-sabotage.

The check is enforceable, not advisory: *"Before returning the answer, silently scan Zone 2 for forbidden lexicon. If any forbidden term appears in Zone 2, rewrite until none remain."*

## Why

Stop letting jargon be a wall. Force the system to prove the chart by cashing it out as human life. Astrology as scaffolding for literature, not as the surface of it.

The contract makes the thesis enforceable: *the reader finishes Zone 2 believing they have been seen as a human, not analysed as a chart. The astrology has become invisible infrastructure.*

## What it unblocked

This is the foundational pattern that propagated across all five voices. Every voice page traces a variant of this contract. The [[topics/the-engine|engine]] thesis ("astrology is the mechanism, literature is the product") becomes a rule, not a vibe.
