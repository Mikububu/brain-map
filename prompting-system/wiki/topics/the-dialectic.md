# The Dialectic

The architectural feature, not a bug. [[apps/1-in-a-billion|1 in a Billion]] and [[apps/unhinged|Unhinged]] are deliberately positioned as opposites. The engine is voice-shape-agnostic; the philosophy is shown through opposition.

## The contrast

| | 1 in a Billion | Unhinged |
|---|---|---|
| Posture | novelist observing | novelist laughing from inside the disaster |
| Conflict rule | tenderness over reverence | provocation over reverence |
| Reader feels | SEEN | SEEN, exposed, grimly amused |
| Body anchor | (abstract narrator) | Henry Miller |
| Tone | consciousness-noir | diagnostic noir / chaos-chic |
| Risk it manages | sentimentality, protective lying | nihilism, cruelty for its own sake |

## What's shared

- Same five-system architecture
- Same Swiss Ephemeris compute
- Same DeepSeek backend
- Same eleven languages
- Most prompt files are byte-identical at import (Unhinged was forked on 2026-04-08, commit `6b4363e`)

## What's different

- The rewrite layer (`prompt-layers/rewrite/narrative-rewrite-en.md`)
- Parts of the style layer, especially the spicy-surreal style guides
- The chaos-comedy override in `styles/production.ts`
- The Henry Miller body anchor in Unhinged's rewrite

## Why dialectic, not contradiction

The user did not build Unhinged because canonical is wrong. They built it because **the engine should be capable of both**, and the difference proves the engine is real (not an accident of voice).

Tenderness with teeth (canonical) and hilarity with diagnosis (Unhinged) are two settings on the same instrument.

## How each voice handles the dialectic

- [[western]]: hardest fork. Henry Miller body anchor, full chaos-comedy override.
- [[vedic]]: full Sanskrit graha names, escalated goddess voice.
- [[kabbalah]]: **no fork.** Imported wholesale. The candle-lit room has nothing to gain from the noir register.
- [[gene-keys]]: dangerous-organism emphasis amplified.
- [[human-design]]: engine pushed to redline.

[[kabbalah]] is the voice that refuses to take sides in the dialectic. Its quietness is itself a position.

## The third position

[[apps/past-life-contracts]] and [[apps/families-app]] are not part of the binary. They are *narrowings* of the engine, not tonal forks: one ruthlessly narrows scope (Vedic-only, two souls); the other broadens scope (1-4 people as one organism). Together with the canonical / Unhinged dialectic, they map the engine's full degrees of freedom.
