# The Five-System Architecture

**2026-02-18**, commit `af0553d2`. The day five distinct narrator consciousnesses were locked in. *"V2 prompt-layer overhaul: 5-system incarnation wiring and generation pipeline sync."*

## Before

A single shared narrator. The pre-v2 production style invoked David Attenborough as the only literary reference (*"David Attenborough narrating human souls"*). All five systems used the same generic *"dark soul storytelling"* template. Voices were not yet differentiated. See [[topics/january-foundation]] for the pre-v2 state.

## What changed

The `backend/prompt-layers/` directory was created (markdown files, replacing the prior TypeScript modules). Five distinct narrator archetypes were declared:

- [[voices/western]], **novelist haunted by the subject**, consciousness noir
- [[voices/vedic]], **Aghori witness at the cremation ground**, patient and unflinching
- [[voices/kabbalah]], **midnight rabbi repairing broken vessels**, kintsugi
- [[voices/gene-keys]], **field biologist studying shadow as organism**, clinical wonder
- [[voices/human-design]], **engineer who fell in love with the machine**, mechanical only

The master document `voice-architecture-all-systems.md` was introduced. ~1900 lines of new voice scaffolding.

## Why

Five astrological systems must sound *genuinely different* or the product is fraud. Each voice gets its own *narrator consciousness*, its own metaphor world, and its own operational rule. The foundation of the systems-differentiation thesis.

## What it unblocked

Every voice page on this wiki sits on top of this commit. The [[topics/the-engine|three-layer architecture]] (System → Voice → Layer 3) takes its mature shape from here. The [[topics/the-dialectic|canonical-vs-Unhinged dialectic]] is later possible because the voice layer is decoupled from the system layer.

This is also the day Anaïs Nin (the named anchor in the v0 incarnation) was abstracted to *"a novelist haunted by the subject"*. Named voice → described consciousness.
