# Start Here

Welcome. This is a brain-map of the four-app suite (1 in a Billion, Unhinged, Past-Life Contracts, Families-app) and the philosophy behind them. **Astrology as literature, not prediction.** 26 pages, all interlinked.

This page is the tour. Read top to bottom and you'll have the whole picture in 15 minutes. Or open the **graph view** (`Cmd + G` on Mac) to see the cluster.

## The big picture in one paragraph

Five astrological systems ([[voices/western|Western]], [[voices/vedic|Vedic]], [[voices/kabbalah|Kabbalah]], [[voices/gene-keys|Gene Keys]], [[voices/human-design|Human Design]]) each rendered through a distinct narrator consciousness. Three composition layers (system → voice → literary rewrite). Eleven languages, each paired with a culturally native literary anchor. **Four apps as four positions on the same engine**: canonical literary noir ([[apps/1-in-a-billion|1 in a Billion]]), diagnostic noir ([[apps/unhinged|Unhinged]]), recovered-memory novella ([[apps/past-life-contracts|Past-Life Contracts]]), family myth ([[apps/families-app|Families-app]]). Underneath all of it, downstream of a 25-year [[topics/forbidden-yoga-context|tantric-retreat practice]], an open infrastructure problem ([[topics/uncensored-llms|uncensored LLMs]]), and the fact that what's being attempted, *Vedic astrology written without flinching, in eleven languages, as literature*, has never been done before.

## What you'll notice

The wiki is built in the user's voice. It quotes the prompts directly. The texture is judicial (mandate, contract, non-negotiable), architectural (rooms, corridors, locked floors), and refuses softness (rescue language is named as something to refuse). This is on purpose. The wiki should sound like the person who built the apps, not like an AI summarising them.

> The reader should feel SEEN, not analysed, not diagnosed.

That's the whole engine, and it's also how to read this wiki.
