# prompting-system index

Mind-map of the four-app prompting philosophy. One line per page, grouped by type.

## Voices
- [[voices/western|Western]], novelist haunted by the subject. Consciousness noir. The foundation; first of the five.
- [[voices/vedic|Vedic]], Aghori witness at the cremation ground. Patient, awed, unflinching.
- [[voices/kabbalah|Kabbalah]], midnight rabbi repairing broken vessels. The quietest voice; light leaks through the cracks.
- [[voices/gene-keys|Gene Keys]], field biologist watching an organism mid-mutation. Built as a defence against Instagram-coach fluff.
- [[voices/human-design|Human Design]], engineer who fell in love with the machine. The most precise voice; refuses moral framing.

(Order of construction: Western → Vedic → Kabbalah → Gene Keys → Human Design.)
