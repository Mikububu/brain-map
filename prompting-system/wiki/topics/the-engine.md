# The Engine

The shared substrate underneath all four apps. Five voices, three layers, eleven languages.

## What it is

A long-narrative astrological reader that treats astrology as scaffolding for literature. The same chart can be rendered in five distinct voice consciousnesses across eleven languages, depending on which app and mode is invoked.

## The five voices

Each is a different *narrator consciousness* with its own metaphor world and **operational rule**:

| Voice | Operational rule |
|---|---|
| [[western]] | Vocabulary FORBIDDEN in Zone 2 |
| [[vedic]] | Same Zone 2 ban + own metaphor world (rivers, fire, neecha) |
| [[kabbalah]] | Vocabulary REQUIRED throughout (reverses Western) |
| [[gene-keys]] | Vocab in body, forbidden in lyrics. Anti-Instagram-coach |
| [[human-design]] | Mechanical only; no moral framing |

Each voice is a different *operational rule on the same engine*.

## The three layers

1. **System** (`prompt-layers/systems/`): terminology, depth model, scope (individual / synastry / incarnation).
2. **Voice** (`prompt-layers/style/style-guide-insert-<system>-voice.md`): turns the system into a narrator consciousness.
3. **Layer 3** (`prompt-layers/rewrite/narrative-rewrite-<lang>.md`): literary transformation in target language with a culturally native voice anchor. See [[topics/layer-3-rewrite]].

Composition flow: **System → Voice → Layer 3**.

## The eleven languages

English, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Mandarin. Each pairs with a native literary anchor (Osho for Hindi, Pelevin for Russian, Yu Hua for Mandarin). See [[topics/language-anchoring]].

## The four apps as tonal forks

Same engine, four expressions:

- [[apps/1-in-a-billion]]: literary consciousness-noir (canonical)
- [[apps/unhinged]]: diagnostic noir (the dialectical opposite)
- [[apps/past-life-contracts]]: recovered-memory novella (Vedic-only)
- [[apps/families-app]]: family myth (relational, 3rd-person)

## What the engine is NOT

This is not an astrology app dressed as literature.

The [[western|Incarnation Zone Contract]] makes it explicit: Zone 1 uses astrology vocabulary; Zone 2 strips it. The chart is source material the reader never sees.

**Astrology is the mechanism. Literature is the product.**
