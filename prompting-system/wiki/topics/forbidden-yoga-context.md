# Forbidden Yoga (Parent Brand)

The four-app suite is downstream of a 25-year practice. The builder, **Michael Wogenburg**, founded [Forbidden Yoga](https://forbidden-yoga.com/) as a high-net-worth tantric retreat brand rooted in **Kashmir Shaivism** and **left-handed Shakta tradition**. Everything in this wiki sits on top of that lineage.

## The brand thesis

> "Forbidden Yoga reflects a core truth about authentic Tantra: it was always a forbidden, transgressive tradition."

Positioned explicitly *against* mainstream wellness and *"diluted neo-tantra."* Retreats in dream-scape settings (Bali, Costa Rica, Mongolia), one client at a time, with placeholder actors (typically 1 to 12 practitioners). Month-long client profiling precedes every retreat. See [[apps/1-in-a-billion]]'s founding story: the yoga clients are the original use-case for the app.

> "Replaced the fundamental element of the Freudian psychoanalytic setting, the couch, with your nude body."

Therapy displaced by embodied practice; the couch by the body. *"Sexual energy work, trauma dissolution, high-performance consciousness training."*

## How it shapes the apps

This context explains a lot of what otherwise might look like edginess for its own sake:

- **The Aghori / Tantra texturing in [[vedic]]** ("naturalist at the cremation ground") is not stylistic flourish. It comes from the builder's own left-handed Shakta lineage.
- **The phrase "left-handed perspective, fatalistic irony"** named in the v0 Vedic writing guide (`418ea42b`, 2026-01-09) is the same left-handed-path tradition that informs the retreats.
- **The [[apps/unhinged|Unhinged]] mission** ("provocation over reverence," sexually explicit, shadow-forward) is structurally the same gesture as Forbidden Yoga's *"forbidden, transgressive tradition"* applied to literature.
- **The [[topics/uncensored-llms|uncensored-LLM research]]** is downstream of the same cultural argument: art and embodied practice have refused censorship for a century; the work is to extend that refusal into AI.
- **The [[topics/the-engine|engine]]'s thesis of astrology-as-literature** is the natural extension of a practice that already treats the body as primary text and consciousness as the thing to be precisely named.

## Why the four apps exist (the real founding logic)

The four apps are not a brand extension. They are **tools the builder wished existed for himself**. From the founding story of [[apps/1-in-a-billion]]:

> "For yoga, I always have a client I analyze months before the project starts, and I consult real astrologers. I paid real people for Vedic astrology and for Gene Keys, among others, and their analyses always took forever and cost a lot of money."

The same logic explains why [[apps/families-app]] (relational scope), [[apps/past-life-contracts]] (Vedic-only contract narratives), and [[apps/unhinged]] (the transgressive register) all make sense as a suite: each tool helps the builder do faster and at higher fidelity what he was already doing manually for his retreat clients.
