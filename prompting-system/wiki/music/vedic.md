# Vedic Music

The music for [[voices/vedic|Vedic]]. Contemporary Indian-classical. **Anoushka Shankar in 2026** is the absolute centre of gravity, locked in by a HARD STYLE LOCK that the user explicitly enforces in the prompt.

## The hard style lock

The prompt says, verbatim:

> If the resulting `minimaxPrompt` does not literally anchor on `Anoushka Shankar in 2026` and explicitly say `contemporary Indian-classical`, **it is wrong**. If there is no clear bansuri spotlight with room to breathe, **it is wrong**.

This is the most enforceable music style anchor in the corpus. The prompt rejects its own output if Anoushka's name and bansuri space do not appear in the brief.

## Synastry variant (overlay)

Anoushka stays the centre. Two birthplace traditions enter as *"secondary and tertiary DNA."* Extended bansuri passages remain essential. Lyrics rule: only Sanskrit and the two person-specific languages (Latin transliteration for Sanskrit, **never Devanagari**). *"Sanskrit should feel like the karmic axis; Person 1 and Person 2 languages should feel like memory answering fate."*

Languages never mix within a line. Language switching is structural, not word-by-word.

## Family-overlay variant (Families-app)

Reframed as family karma, inheritance, duty, grief, blessing, transmission. **No romance language.** Sonic centre stays Anoushka alone (simpler than synastry; no crossover). Traditional instruments (sitar, bansuri, tanpura, tabla) remain, kept *"concise and evocative rather than technical."*

## Dead ends

- Bowie / Eno layering references (removed Mar 22)
- Generic "operatic" or modern Indian fusion directions (replaced with TRADITIONAL / FOLK + ethno ambient)
- Bansuri spotlight as soft preference (hardened to non-negotiable)
- Devanagari script in Sanskrit lyrics (banned; Latin transliteration only)
- Mixing languages within a single line (banned; structural switching only)

## Philosophy

Vedic music is **fate made audible**. Anoushka Shankar's meditative depth (sitar lead, tanpura drone, tabla pulse, bansuri passages that breathe space) applied to nakshatra, dasha, karma language. The song is a 4-minute arc, not a commercial pop snippet, structured to sustain instrumental passages that feel like ritual. Sanskrit and regional languages form a triadic vocal texture, never blended.
