## Synastry variant (overlay)

Two unnamed voices trading fragments. Cultural crossover under the 50/25/25 rule. The two birthplace traditions are referenced as flavour, not formula. The MiniMax brief emphasises a 4-5 minute arc with instrumental breathing room.

## Family-overlay variant (Families-app)

Reframed entirely. **No romance, no seduction, no dating, no sexual chemistry, no couple language.** Family gravity replaces romantic longing. Same Sienna Rose anchor, same 25-35 line structure, but lyrics about *"lineage, memory, pressure, protection, distance, and what the bond does to the wider family field."*

## Dead ends

- Artist-list anchoring (Radiohead, Bon Iver, FKA Twigs, Sigur Rós, etc.) tried briefly, removed
- Bowie cut-up algorithmic framing (replaced with "surreal storytelling")
- Name injection into lyrics (banned)
- LLM cultural research without guardrails (replaced by TRADITIONAL / FOLK mandate)
- Instruments lists in MiniMax brief (replaced by concise style / mood language)

Indie-pop as introspection. The song is a personal confession using astrological texture, not an explanation of the chart.
