# Kabbalah

Third of the five voices. The quietest. After [[western]] (the novelist) and [[vedic]] (the Aghori), this one is a candle in a cracked room.

## Now

> A midnight rabbi repairing broken vessels in the dark. Broken vessels hold the most light, because light leaks through the cracks.

The operational rule reverses [[western]]: Kabbalistic vocabulary is *mandatory* throughout, embedded as architecture, never as textbook definition. Forbidden: *"In Kabbalah, Chesed represents mercy."* Required: *"His Chesed runs unchecked, the mercy with no Gevurah to contain it."*

## Concepts in use vs. dropped

In current prompts:

- All ten **Sephiroth** (Keter through Malkhut)
- **Three Pillars** (Mercy, Severity, Middle)
- **Tikkun** (recurring behavioural loop, not "homework")
- **Klipoth** (protective shell that outlived its purpose, not demonic)
- **Four Worlds** (Atziluth, Beriah, Yetzirah, Assiyah, for capacity)
- **Gilgul** (reincarnation as draft revision)
- **Hebrew letter signature** (Sun, Moon, Rising as consonants)

Tried then dropped or never wired:

- **Gematria** (explicitly banned across all versions)
- **Tikkun Olam** (collective repair, absent)
- **Paths on the Tree** (never mapped)
- **Hermetic vs. Lurianic** distinction (not made)

## Dead ends

- Zone 2 vocabulary ban (reversed on `c1abc2de`, the system's biggest philosophical pivot)
- Hebrew-only music lyrics (failed singability)
- Tikkun as "spiritual assignment" / homework (replaced by behavioural-loop framing)
- Klipoth as evil or pathology (replaced by tenderness)
- Invented Sephirotic placements (banned at the boundary)

## Texture

How this builder thinks about Kabbalah:

- Vessels and cracks: kintsugi as the core metaphor; light leaks through the breaks
- Multi-floor house: the Tree grows up toward heaven and down into the underworld, simultaneously
- Shells and bodyguards: protection that outlived its purpose; the shell that became the self
- Rooms that are always cold; doors that never open
- Short sentences after colon; restatement instead of definition
- Adverbial specificity: *"the mercy floods the room because the person does not know how to love at normal volume"*

Refused: mystical jargon for its own sake, fortune-telling Gilgul, shadow pathologisation, textbook definitions, code-switching mid-line.

The paradox: the narrator whispers. The quietest voice is also the most technically saturated. Intimacy through precise naming, not through hiding.
